I honestly don’t even know what a blog is supposed to be. I just saw something similar online and thought, that looks cool why not make one for myself too?


Until now, my habit has been taking videos to capture moments. Editing them made time feel like time-travel, which I loved. But lately, I’ve felt the need for something different. My thoughts are always scattered, my life has become pretty unordered, and I’m craving some kind of system - an order.


So I decided: why not try writing things down? A daily log. A place where I can put whatever feels meaningful YouTube videos, podcasts, articles, something I learned, or even just a random thought that feels cool. Maybe even one interesting thing I did each day, so no day goes forgotten.


I also like the idea of a “personalized AI”—an AI that knows your thoughts and memories. I’m not going to put everything here, but this blog can still be a part of that idea.


As for today: I completed the chat interface with RAG using Postgres and Voyage AI. I found some nice UI inspirations online, adapted them into mine, and also added Notion integration to make it easy to write these entries.


So yeah—this is Day 1 of the blog. I don’t know if this is a good blog yet, but hopefully I’ll figure it out with time, and write more properly as I go.
