Me and Sahal reworked our cold call approach today. Until now, we were just bluntly asking, _“Do you need a website?”_ — which shut doors quickly with people who didn’t. Instead, we shifted to positioning ourselves as offering **custom software solutions**, tailoring each pitch to the niche or category we were targeting.


The difference showed fast: after running many demo calls between ourselves, when Sahal moved into actual calls, his tone and delivery had improved drastically. It felt like progress in motion. Now we wait to see how prospects respond.


Later, I left for Kochi for the KPH hackathon. I wanted to grab a shawarma for dinner, but the city had other plans — rain, traffic, and then the bus arriving earlier than expected. No shawarma. Still, onwards to Kochi anyway.
