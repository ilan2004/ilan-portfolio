Lately, I’ve been spending time on DSA practice and solving LeetCode questions. Alongside that, I’ve been exploring hackathons because I want to experience building under pressure and collaborating on new ideas.


What I’ve realized is this: coming up with a good idea often feels harder than executing it. The desire to widen my world and explore more opportunities is strong, but sometimes I find myself stuck — not because of a lack of effort, but because finding the right direction isn’t always straightforward.


On top of that, I keep asking myself: am I really using my time optimally? Am I spending it in the right places? That question keeps repeating in my mind lately. Maybe it’s a sign of ambition, or maybe it’s just the pressure of wanting to move faster. Either way, it pushes me to think harder about what I’m doing and why.


I think this tension is part of the journey. Learning to sit with that “stuck” feeling, keep experimenting, and keep looking for sparks is probably just as important as building skills.
