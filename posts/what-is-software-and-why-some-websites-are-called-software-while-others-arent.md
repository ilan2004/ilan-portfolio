What is software? Even as a software engineering student, I had this doubt: why don’t we call some websites _software_, but for others we do?


Here’s what I found.


---


## What Makes Something Software?

- A set of **instructions** that tells hardware what to do.
- **Intangible**, but executable by a machine.
- Built with **code + logic** to solve problems or process data.

---


## Is a Website Software?


Yes. But there’s a difference in how people refer to them:

- **Static website** → Mostly information (like a digital brochure). Technically software, but feels more like a document.
- **Dynamic website (Web App)** → Has logic, accounts, dashboards, payments, etc. Clearly _software_.

---


## Why the Distinction Matters

- **“Just a website”** → Info display, lower value.
- **“Software / Web App”** → Solves problems, higher value, business impact.

---


## In Short

- All websites are software.
- People say “website” when it’s only showing info.
- People say “software” when it actually does work.
