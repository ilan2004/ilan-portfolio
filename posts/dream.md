2025 so far has been about building momentum. I’ve realized that growth doesn’t come from sudden breakthroughs but from stacking consistent efforts over time. Right now, my focus is divided into three main lanes: skills, projects, and career direction.

### Skills

* Sharpening fundamentals in **DSA and JavaScript**, working through LeetCode and small problems daily.
* Exploring **Next.js, React, and Tailwind CSS** deeper by building production-level projects.
* Learning the basics of **cloud computing and ML projects**, preparing for bigger opportunities.

### Projects

* **StarShape**: refining the service catalog and building credibility with clients, while keeping it lean and manageable.
* **WHETRIP**: a VR/360 tourism platform concept, experimenting with features like the Hidden Gems Map and VR hotel previews.
* Smaller freelance projects: practical work that helps sharpen skills and builds reputation.

### Career Direction

* Goal is to secure an **overseas SDE role** after graduation (2026–2029 window).
* Building a **portfolio** that’s more than just code — projects that demonstrate problem-solving, creativity, and polish.
* Strengthening **online presence** through blogging, portfolio updates, and consistent output.

### The Feeling Right Now

I’m in the middle of the grind — not at the finish line, but steadily moving. Some days it feels slow, but I can sense the trajectory improving. Each project, each solved problem, each blog post adds to the compounding effect.

This stage is less about perfection and more about **showing up, learning fast, and shipping consistently**. That’s the mindset I’m carrying forward.
