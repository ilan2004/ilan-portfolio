<u>What is</u> <u>**hashtable**</u> 


A **hashtable** (or hashmap) is a data structure that stores information in **key-value pairs**, kind of like a dictionary. Instead of searching through a list one by one, a hashtable uses a **hash function** to instantly figure out where a key should be stored in memory, which makes operations like lookup, insertion, and deletion super fast—on average, just **O(1) time complexity**. This efficiency is why hashtables are so widely used in programming, from counting frequencies in arrays to powering caches and databases.
