It’s been a while since I’ve written anything. A lot has been happening, and I think I’ll probably post everything day by day, because every day recently has carried its own story.


Right now, at this very moment, I just feel alive.


Like I genuinely love being here, in this life. There’s so much I want to do: build apps, understand markets, learn how distribution works. I’m in that phase where curiosity feels endless.


I made some money recently, through a bit of work that came exactly when I needed it, the right thing at the right time. For the longest time, I thought life was all about finding the road, choosing the car, checking the map, and driving alone. But lately, it’s like things have started to align. People, opportunities, timing — everything is just happening, and it feels amazing.


I’ve always been a people person. Whenever someone asks what I want to do in life, my answer’s simple: I want to meet a lot of people. A lot of amazing people.


And recently, I actually did. Each one had something different to teach me, and in the process, I picked up new dreams, fresh ones that I hadn’t even thought of before.


And I think that’s how I know I’m living the right way.


The pauses in life aren’t empty. They’re just the calm before a full-packed experience.
