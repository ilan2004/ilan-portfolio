Lately, this question has been circling in my head: _“Who are you?”_


I can say whatever I feel I am—but in reality, what defines me isn’t what I say, it’s what I do. As Batman put it: _“It’s not who I am underneath, but what I do that defines me.”_


I’m starting to realize that a portfolio isn’t just optional; it’s a necessity. If you want to be part of the ecosystem you’re aiming for, people need to see who you are, and that has to be visible in what you’ve built. My GitHub, my LinkedIn, my projects—all of this together becomes my definition in practice.


That’s why I want to shift my focus toward building a portfolio that speaks for me clearly. The hackathon project _Nudge_ will be the start.


_Nudge_ (previously _MindShift_) is a hackathon MVP website aimed at helping users [shift their mindset / track habits / make small behavioral changes]. The platform’s goal is to [solve the core problem: e.g., make positive changes easier to adopt or help people reflect and act].


This project marks the first step in building a portfolio that clearly shows what I do and who I am as a builder.
